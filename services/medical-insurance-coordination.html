Medical & Insurance Coordination

At DCMS MM2H, we provide comprehensive Medical & Insurance Coordination services to help expatriates, investors, and families access medical care, understand insurance policies, and stay fully protected. Our goal is to make your healthcare journey smooth, safe, and worry-free.

Medical & Insurance Coordination is a personalized service where we assist clients in navigating Malaysia's healthcare and insurance systems. From selecting the right insurance plan to scheduling medical appointments, we ensure you receive the care you need without confusion or delay.

How we help

Insurance plan selection

We compare medical insurance options suited to MM2H holders and their dependents.

Medical examination coordination

We arrange the medical check-ups required as part of your MM2H application.

Family coverage planning

We help structure coverage for spouses, children, and parents included in your application.

Ongoing coordination

We remain a point of contact for insurance renewals and claims questions after you've settled in.
