MM2H New Application

A Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) application is far more than filling out forms; it is a high-stakes legal and financial submission governed by the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture (MOTAC) and the Immigration Department of Malaysia.

We manage the full new-application process for every MM2H category, so your submission is accurate and complete the first time.

Document application checklist (phased preparation)

Helpful tip
  • If accompanied by spouse: marriage certificate (notarized English translation and double authentication / legalization).
  • If accompanied by children: birth certificate (notarized English translation and double authentication / legalization).
  • If accompanied by parents: proof of relationship / marriage certificate (notarized English translation and double authentication / legalization).
  • If accompanied by in-laws: proof of relationship between spouse and parents / marriage certificate (notarized English translation and double authentication / legalization).
  • Resume/CV and employment verification letter required for both the main applicant and spouse.
1
Phase 1 — pre-approval documents
Main applicant's passport
Color scan of the bio-data page.
Dependents' passports
Color scan of the bio-data page for all accompanying family members.
Recent passport photographs
Blue background — 2 physical copies per person, or digital copies.
Employment verification letter
Main applicant / spouse, if applicable.
Bank statements
Most recent 3 months.
Payslips
Most recent 3 months.
Curriculum vitae (resume)
Main applicant and accompanying family members.
Proof of relationship
Marriage certificate (spouse), birth certificate (children), or adoption certificate, where applicable.
Certificate of single status
For dependent children aged 21 to 34, with proof of unemployed status.
Certificate of good conduct
CGC / LOGC, statutory declaration, or police clearance — required for all participants aged 18 and above.
Additional supporting documents
Any relevant documents required upon further review.
Note: All documents translated into English must be authenticated or legalized by the Embassy, High Commission, or Consulate of Malaysia, or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Malaysia.
2
Phase 2 — post-approval documents
Medical insurance policy
Applicable for participants aged 60 and below — local or overseas policy with global coverage.
Medical examination report
Form MF II — health check issued by an authorized clinic or hospital in Malaysia.
Fixed deposit certificate
Proof of placement in a Malaysian bank.
Proof of property purchase
If applicable.
Important notes
  • All document copies must be certified by an Embassy, High Commission, or Notary Public and a registered Commissioner for Oaths in Malaysia.
  • If the original documents are not in English, they must be translated by a qualified professional translator and officially certified.

MM2H application process

Application submission
1 · Initial consultation
Consult on programme details and select the package that fits your needs.
2 · Agreement signing & payment
Sign the agency authorization agreement and settle the 20% deposit.
3 · Document preparation & legalization
Gather and legalize passports, financial proof, and family documents.
4 · Document submission for review
All documents are formally submitted to MOTAC for approval.
Government approval
5 · Conditional approval issued
Once granted, prepare for visa endorsement and settle the remaining balance.
Visa & arrival
6 · Travel preparation for Malaysia
Enter Malaysia within 3 months of the Conditional Approval Letter.
7 · Arrival in Malaysia
Open a bank account, place the fixed deposit, complete your medical exam, and buy insurance.
8 · Completion
Successfully collect and endorse your MM2H pass / visa.
