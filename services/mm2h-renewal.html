MM2H Renewal

With our expert MM2H renewal services, we help you renew your visa in Malaysia smoothly, on time, and without unnecessary delays. Whether your pass is nearing expiry or you're unsure about the latest requirements, our team ensures your renewal process is handled with precision and care.

At DCMS MM2H Renewal Services, we focus on accuracy, transparency, and personalised support so you can continue enjoying your life in Malaysia with complete peace of mind.

How renewal works

01

Renewal eligibility review

We check your fixed deposit standing, stay-day compliance, and any changes to your dependents before you renew.

02

Document update

We refresh supporting documents that may have expired since your original approval, such as medical reports or bank statements.

03

Fee payment

We calculate your exact renewal fee based on your category and process payment through the correct channel.

04

Renewal approval

We track your renewal through to approval and confirm your new validity period.

Renewal fees by category

  • Platinum — RM5,000
  • Gold — RM3,000
  • Silver — RM1,500
  • SEZ / SFZ — RM300

Figures per the official One Stop Centre MM2H schedule and subject to change.
