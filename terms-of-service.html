These terms govern the use of DCMS Social Publisher ("the Tool"), an internal content-publishing tool operated by DCMS (MM2H) Sdn Bhd ("DCMS").

1. Purpose

The Tool lets authorised DCMS staff prepare DCMS's own photos and videos with captions and publish them to social media accounts owned or managed by DCMS, including uploading videos to a TikTok account's inbox as drafts for the account holder to review and post.

2. Who may use it

Only DCMS staff authorised by DCMS management. Access is restricted to approved Telegram accounts. The Tool is not available to the public.

3. Connecting a TikTok account

  • Only the owner or an authorised manager of a TikTok account may connect it.
  • Connecting is voluntary and can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app or by contacting DCMS.
  • Every post is shown to a DCMS staff member for approval before anything is uploaded; TikTok drafts are posted only by the account holder in the TikTok app.

  • Only content that DCMS owns or has the rights to may be published.
