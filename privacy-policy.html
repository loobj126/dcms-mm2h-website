Privacy Policy

How DCMS (MM2H) Sdn Bhd collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DCMS (MM2H) Sdn Bhd (Company No. 202501033290 (1634700-T)) ("DCMS", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal data when you visit www.dcmsgroup.com.my (the "Website"), contact us, or interact with our pages on social media platforms including TikTok, Facebook and Instagram. We process personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia ("PDPA") and its amendments.

1. Who we are

DCMS (MM2H) Sdn Bhd is an advisory company that assists families and investors with the Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) programme, including new applications, renewals, banking assistance, medical and insurance coordination, and property sourcing.

2. Personal data we collect

a) Information you give us

When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email or social media, or engage our services, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details, such as your name, nationality, telephone number, email address and country of residence;
  • Information you share about your MM2H enquiry or application, such as your family composition, financial and employment information, and supporting documents (for example passport copies, bank statements, certificates and photographs);
  • The content of messages and correspondence you send us.

We only collect the documents and details needed to advise on, prepare or support your application or enquiry.

b) Information collected automatically when you use the Website

  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and date and time of access, for security and reliability.
  • Visitor count. We keep an anonymous running total of Website visits. The count is a number only and is not linked to you personally.
  • Language preference. Your chosen display language is stored in your browser's session storage so that pages stay in your language while you browse. It is cleared when you close the tab or browser.

The Website does not use advertising cookies and does not build profiles of visitors for advertising purposes.

3. How we use your personal data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotations and advisory services;
  • To prepare, submit and follow up MM2H applications, renewals and related arrangements on your instructions;
  • To coordinate with banks, insurers, clinics, legal practitioners and property professionals where you ask us to;
  • To keep records and meet legal, regulatory and accounting obligations;
  • To operate, secure and improve the Website;
  • To communicate with you about our services where you have contacted us or agreed to hear from us.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share your data with

We share personal data only when needed for the purposes above, and only with:

  • Government agencies and authorities involved in the MM2H programme, where required to process your application;
  • Banks, insurers, medical providers, lawyers, translators and property professionals, where you ask us to involve them;
  • Service providers that help us run the Website and communicate with you, such as our web hosting provider (Vercel) and messaging and email services;
  • Professional advisers, and regulators or law enforcement where we are legally required to do so.

Service providers may process data only to perform services for us and are expected to keep it secure.

Third-party content on the Website

  • Google Maps. The Contact Us page embeds a Google Map. When it loads, Google may collect technical information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp. If you tap the WhatsApp button, you leave the Website and your chat is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

5. Social media, including TikTok

We maintain pages and accounts on social media platforms such as TikTok, Facebook and Instagram to share information about MM2H. When you view, follow, comment on, message or otherwise interact with our accounts, the platform operator collects and uses your data under its own privacy policy, and acts as an independent controller of that data. We may see information the platform makes available to account owners, such as your public profile name, comments, messages and aggregated analytics, and we use it only to respond to you and understand how our content performs.

We do not ask you to share passport details, financial documents or other sensitive information through public comments or social media direct messages. If we need such documents, we will ask you to send them through a more suitable channel.

6. Overseas transfers

Because MM2H applicants live around the world and some of our service providers operate internationally, your personal data may be transferred to and stored in countries outside Malaysia. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure the data remains protected in line with the PDPA.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to provide our services, to comply with legal, regulatory and accounting requirements, and to handle disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Security

We take reasonable practical steps to protect your personal data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction, including restricting access to personnel who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • Withdraw your consent, or limit how we process your data, subject to legal and contractual requirements (withdrawal may affect our ability to continue providing services);
  • Ask how to make an enquiry or complaint about how we handle your data.

To exercise these rights, email us at mm2h@dcmktgsolution.com with your name and contact details and a description of your request. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the period required by law.

10. Children

The Website is not directed at children under 18. Where an application includes a child's information, it should be provided by a parent or legal guardian.

11. Links to other sites

The Website may link to external websites, such as official government portals and social media pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with its effective date.
