Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DCMS (MM2H) Sdn Bhd ("DCMS", "we", "us") handles personal data on our website dcmsgroup.com.my and in DCMS Social Publisher, our internal tool for publishing DCMS's own marketing content to our social media accounts (including TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and Xiaohongshu). We follow the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia).

1. Who uses DCMS Social Publisher

The tool is used only by authorised DCMS staff to publish content to social media accounts owned or managed by DCMS. It is not offered to the public.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

When an authorised staff member connects a TikTok account, TikTok shares, with that person's permission:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and profile picture, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos chosen by DCMS staff to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts (scope video.upload), or to publish them where the account holder has chosen to do so.

We do not collect TikTok followers, messages, viewing history, other users' data, or any data beyond the above.

3. How we use the data

  • To upload and publish DCMS's own videos and photos, with captions written for DCMS, to the connected account.
  • To show the posting status to DCMS staff.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on DCMS's own computer system, in files readable only by the posting service, and are never shown in chats or logs. Media files are kept on DCMS's systems; photos sent to Instagram/Facebook may be held temporarily (deleted automatically within 1 day) on our cloud storage so those platforms can fetch them.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep TikTok tokens only while the account is connected. You can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app (Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or by emailing us; we then delete the stored tokens and profile data within 30 days. Records of what was posted (date, platform, file name, link) are kept for our business records.

6. Website visitors

Information you send us through our website or by email/phone (such as your name, contact details and MM2H enquiry) is used only to respond to and handle your enquiry.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.
